# Foundation Models

Foundation models are large pretrained models that can be adapted to many tasks.

Characteristics:
- Trained on broad, diverse datasets
- Useful for transfer learning and few-shot learning
- Common in NLP, vision, and multimodal AI

Examples:
- GPT, BERT, PaLM in language
- CLIP, DINO, Vision Transformers in vision
- Multimodal models combining text, image, and audio

Usage:
- Fine-tune on specific downstream tasks
- Use prompts or adapters for task customization
- Evaluate for bias, safety, and robustness
